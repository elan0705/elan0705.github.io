SYS.01 / ABOUT

Who I Am

I'm a roboticist and researcher with a deep interest in robotics, human cognition and medical electronics — drawn to the space where nature, technology and human behaviour meet.

I enjoy building working prototypes rather than staying purely theoretical, and I bring a strong research-and-development instinct to every project, paired with management, leadership and collaborative habits picked up across academic and engineering teams.

My background spans cognitive robotics and medical electronics, which is why my work keeps circling back to a single question: how do you design a machine that genuinely understands, assists and works with a human — not just near one.

SYS.02 / RESEARCH & DESIGN

Research & Design

Six interconnected areas run through everything I build — not a skills list, more like the standing benches in a lab.

01

Humanoid Robotics

Human-like reaching, grasping and articulated movement, built around the geometry of the human arm.

02

Human–Robot Interaction

Understanding human behaviour first, then designing robotic systems that fit around it.

03

Prosthetics

Affordable, adjustable, human-centred prosthetic design — built to adapt as a person's body changes.

04

Cognitive Robotics

Connecting robotic systems to models of human cognition and behaviour.

05

Motion & Gaze

Motion capture and eye-tracking, synchronised in space to study how humans move and look.

06

Medical Robotics

Robotics applied to medical and assistive technology, from telesurgery to rehabilitation.
